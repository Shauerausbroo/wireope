https://github.com/project-lighter/CT-FM
  name     : CT-FM, a 3D image-based foundation model for computed tomography
  licence  : MIT
  role     : frozen volumetric encoder that produces the lumen envelope (Sec. 3.2)
  source   : cited by the manuscript, footnote 1 of Sec. 3.2
  access   : open repository, no registration and no data-use agreement

https://registry.opendata.aws/msd/
  name     : Medical Segmentation Decathlon, Task 09 (hepatic vasculature)
  licence  : Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  role     : open auxiliary corpus for pretraining and external segmentation comparison
  source   : cited by the manuscript, footnote 2 of Sec. 3.2
  access   : anonymous access with --no-sign-request; staged for MONAI at s3://msd-for-monai

https://medmnist.com/
  name     : MedMNIST v2, 3D subset VesselMNIST3D
  licence  : Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
  role     : lightweight vessel-geometry probe on small volumes
  source   : cited by the manuscript, footnote 3 of Sec. 3.2
  access   : open; the benchmark states its source modality is magnetic resonance angiography
             and that it is not intended for clinical use

https://zenodo.org/records/10390295
  name     : ARCADE (Automatic Region-based Coronary Artery Disease diagnostics)
  licence  : Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal
  role     : lesion-labelling auxiliary set
  source   : cited by the manuscript, footnote 4 of Sec. 3.2 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10390295)
  access   : open; the record describes x-ray angiography images rather than the CTA volumes the
             framework conditions on, so it enters no clinical claim

https://github.com/Project-MONAI/model-zoo
  name     : MONAI Model Zoo, Swin UNETR segmentation bundle
  licence  : Apache License 2.0
  role     : universal supervised segmentation baseline
  source   : cited by the manuscript, footnote 5 of Sec. 3.2
  access   : open repository; the bundle downloads by a single command with no registration
